# Aetheris heroes

Eleven heroes, weakest to strongest. A new player picks one of the four
starters with their 50-peso registration fee; the rest are bought in the shop.

## Art brief

The current images are finished **cards**: a circular bust in an ornate frame
with the hero name printed on it, about 256px square. They cannot be cropped
or extended into full bodies.

For the full-body art the app needs, per hero:

- **one tall transparent PNG**, roughly 3:4 or 9:16, 1024px tall or more
- **no frame and no text** - the app draws the frame and the name
- **standing neutral pose**, facing slightly inward, full figure with feet
- consistent light direction and eye level across all eleven, so they look
  like one set when two of them face each other in the arena
- a little headroom and floor space, so a recoil or lunge animation has
  somewhere to move

Name them `body-<id>.png` using the ids below and drop them in the repo root.
The page falls back to the existing card art wherever one is missing.
